Still — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 10, 2026
App: Still (com.elunexa.still.rescue)
Applies to: Still 1.0.0
Privacy contact: elunexalabs@gmail.com

Information processed on your device

Still provides daily smoke-free commitments and a one-minute guided breathing session.

When you record a daily commitment, Still saves that calendar date on your device. It uses these dates to show whether you have committed today and to calculate your total recorded commitments. Still also saves your language and appearance preferences locally.

Commitment dates can reveal a health-related habit. They are used to provide the commitment feature, rather than to create a medical profile. Still does not request your name, email address, phone number, location, medical records or physiological measurements. It does not maintain a persistent history of rescue sessions or responses to breathing sessions.

Transmission and third-party services

Still 1.0.0 does not send your commitment records or settings to Elunexa Labs servers or to advertising, analytics or crash-reporting services. It does not include Firebase, Analytics, Crashlytics, advertising SDKs or a third-party data collection service. It does not offer an account, cloud synchronization, data export or sharing feature.

Still uses Android and AndroidX components for its interface, local storage and widgets. These components are not used by Still to upload your records. The app does not request the Android INTERNET permission.

Widgets and permissions

When you add a Still widget, the current commitment status is supplied to your device's home-screen launcher for display. People who can see your home screen may see that status. You can remove the widget using your launcher.

Still uses the RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED permission to refresh widgets after the device restarts. It does not use this permission to transmit data.

Storage, retention and deletion

Records and preferences are stored in app-private storage protected by Android's normal app isolation. Still does not implement additional application-level encryption.

Commitment dates remain until you delete the app's local data. Still does not automatically expire them and does not provide an in-app record deletion or reset button.

To delete local data, open Android Settings → Apps → Still → Storage (or Storage & cache) → Clear data / Clear storage. The wording varies by device. A normal uninstall also removes Still's local app data. Removing a widget alone does not delete your records. Clearing data also resets your saved preferences.

Elunexa Labs cannot retrieve or delete these locally stored records remotely because Still does not upload them.

Operating-system backup and device transfer

Still is configured to disable Android cloud backup and does not provide its own backup or cloud-sync service. Device-to-device transfer and restoration performed by Android or a device manufacturer can vary. Existing system transfer rules may permit transfer of some local settings. This policy does not promise that every device manufacturer's migration tool will leave all app data on the original device.

Deleting Still's local data does not necessarily delete copies previously made by a separate system or migration service. Those copies must be managed through that service's controls.

Health-related use

Still processes smoke-free commitment dates locally as described above. It provides general breathing support; it is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent a medical condition. It does not access Health Connect or medical records.

Contact and changes

For privacy questions, email elunexalabs@gmail.com . If you contact us through a separate email or support service, information you voluntarily send is handled through that service, outside Still's offline data flow. Avoid sending sensitive health information.

If a future version changes how Still handles data, this policy will be updated with a new effective date.
