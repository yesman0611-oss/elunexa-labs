隱私權政策（繁體中文）

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 20 日。開發者：Elunexa Labs。適用 App：Elunexa Notes（com.elunexa.notes）。

記事與本機資料

記事、分類及設定主要儲存於裝置本機。開發者不提供自有的記事雲端同步服務，不會將記事內容提供給廣告服務。

備份與匯出

您可以自行選擇備份檔案的保存位置。若啟用 Android 系統備份或裝置轉移功能，部分應用程式資料可能依系統及備份服務設定進行備份或轉移。

廣告與同意

免費版可能顯示 Google AdMob 廣告，Pro 版本移除廣告。Google AdMob 與 Google User Messaging Platform（UMP）可能依所在地區、同意選擇及裝置設定，處理裝置及網路資訊、廣告互動、廣告識別碼或其他適用識別資訊，以及廣告同意狀態。詳見 Google 隱私權政策。

應用程式內購買

Pro 永久買斷由 Google Play Billing 處理。完整付款卡片資訊由 Google 處理；應用程式會查詢購買狀態以提供與恢復 Pro 權益。

評分

應用程式可能透過 Google Play In-App Review 或商店連結邀請評分。評論與評分由 Google Play 處理。

保留與刪除

本機記事與設定通常保留至您刪除、清除應用程式資料或解除安裝。匯出的備份檔案需由您自行在儲存位置刪除。Google Play 購買紀錄、廣告資料及系統備份依相應服務的政策管理。

安全、兒童及更新

我們採取合理措施減少不必要的資料存取，但無法保證任何儲存方式絕對安全。本應用程式並非專為兒童設計。如資料處理方式有重大變更，本政策將更新。

Privacy Policy (English)

Effective October 20, 2026. Developer: Elunexa Labs. App: Elunexa Notes (com.elunexa.notes).

Notes and local data

Notes, categories and preferences are primarily stored on your device. We do not operate a proprietary notes cloud sync service and do not supply note contents to advertising services.

Backup and export

You choose where exported backup files are stored. Android system backup or device transfer, if enabled, may back up or transfer some app data according to system and provider settings.

Advertising and consent

The free version may display Google AdMob ads; Pro removes ads. Google AdMob and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) may process device and network information, advertising interactions, advertising identifiers or other applicable identifiers, and consent choices, depending on your region, settings and consent. See the Google Privacy Policy.

In-app purchases

Lifetime Pro purchases are handled by Google Play Billing. Google handles full payment card details. The app checks purchase status to grant and restore Pro access.

Reviews

The app may invite ratings via Google Play In-App Review or a store link. Google Play handles ratings and reviews.

Retention and deletion

Local notes and settings generally remain until you delete them, clear app data, or uninstall. You must separately delete any exported backups. Google Play purchase records, advertising data, and system backups are governed by their respective providers.

Security, children, and changes

We take reasonable steps to limit unnecessary data access, but no storage method is perfectly secure. The app is not specifically designed for children. We will update this policy when our data practices materially change.
